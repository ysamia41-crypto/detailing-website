About Me

Meet Tre

My name is Tre, the detailer guy from HTX, and I’m a 25-year-old professional detailer with over six years of experience in the auto detailing industry. My passion for detailing began at a young age when my grandfather taught me the skills and pride that go into properly caring for a vehicle. What started as a hobby quickly became a lifelong passion.

Since then, I’ve dedicated myself to perfecting my craft and delivering high-quality results with every vehicle I service. Becoming a mobile detailer has always been my dream, and today I’m proud to be turning that dream into reality by providing convenient, reliable, and professional detailing services directly to my customers.
